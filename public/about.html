ℹ️ Scientific Methodology & Official Data Sources

BhumiRakshak synthesizes live open meteorological modeling with satellite-derived historical landslide databases and geological susceptibility zonation.

🌧️

Open-Meteo Weather API

Real-Time & Historical Atmospheric Data

Supplies changing environmental variables without proprietary API barriers:

  • Past 24h Rainfall: Immediate trigger intensity (mm).
  • Past 72h Antecedent Rain: Critical multi-day slope soaking accumulation.
  • Forecast Next 24h: Short-term cloudburst and storm threats.
  • Multi-Layer Soil Moisture: Volumetric water content across 0–1cm, 1–3cm, and 3–9cm root zone layers.

ISRO Landslide Atlas of India

National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC, 2023)

Published by the Department of Space, Government of India:

  • 80,000 Mapped Landslides (1998–2022): Spatial database of recorded mass movements across the Himalayas and Western Ghats.
  • District Vulnerability Rankings: Top-ranked districts including Rudraprayag, Tehri Garhwal, Thrissur, Rajouri, Wayanad, Shimla, Chamoli, and Darjeeling.
  • Historical Proximity Metric: Evaluates proximity to chronic landslide scars.

🏔️

Geological Survey of India (GSI)

National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM)

India's nodal agency for landslide hazard investigations:

  • Macro-Hazard Zonation: Categorizes terrain into Very High, High, Moderate, and Low susceptibility zones based on slope gradient, lithology, and fault proximity.
  • Himalayan Shearing Zones: Accounting for fragile phyllites, weathered schists, and active fault lines.

🚨 Mandatory Disclaimer & Disaster Management Guidance

Important Safety Notice: This website provides an estimated landslide risk indicator combining open meteorological variables, satellite inventories, and community reports. It does not make deterministic claims that a specific hillside will collapse at a given second.

This platform is not an official evacuation directive. In all cases of extreme monsoons, cloudbursts, or suspected slope failures, citizens must strictly adhere to evacuation notices and alerts issued by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA), and the local District Magistrate.
