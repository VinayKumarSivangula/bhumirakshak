        Data Sources, Guidance & Ethical Disclaimer

        Important Notice: This website provides an estimated landslide risk assessment calculated by fusing real-time meteorological variables from Open-Meteo with the ISRO-NRSC Landslide Atlas of India (1998–2022), Geological Survey of India (GSI) macro-zonation, and community ground observations.

        This platform does not make deterministic claims that a landslide will definitely occur at a specific second, nor is it an official government evacuation order unless sanctioned by disaster management authorities. For official emergency evacuations, strictly follow orders issued by your local District Magistrate, State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA), and the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA).

        Data Citations: Open-Meteo Weather API • ISRO Landslide Atlas of India (NRSC 2023) • Bhuvan / ISRO Geo-Platform • Geological Survey of India (GSI) • NDMA India
