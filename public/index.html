        BhumiRakshak (भूमि रक्षक)

        A scientific Himalayan disaster early warning and slope risk platform designed for everyday citizens, local panchayats, and disaster response authorities.

        This system provides estimated risk indicators based on open scientific data and does not replace official evacuation orders from NDMA/SDMA.
